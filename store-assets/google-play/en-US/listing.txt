App name
FreddyFridge

Short description
Track food expiry dates, get reminders and reduce waste from your fridge.

Full description
Keep track of the food in your fridge and use it before it expires.

FreddyFridge gives you a clear view of products expiring today, coming up next, already consumed, or expired. Add an item with its expiry date, then let configurable reminders help you stay ahead of waste.

KEY FEATURES

• Track food and expiry dates in one place
• See what expires today and in the coming days
• Receive configurable expiry reminders
• Add product names quickly with optional voice input
• Mark products as opened or consumed
• Review consumed and expired product history
• Share useful product lists
• Choose reminder timing from the app settings

DESIGNED FOR EVERYDAY USE

The color-coded lists make urgent products easy to recognize. FreddyFridge stores your food records locally on your device, keeping the experience focused and straightforward.

Whether you are planning meals, checking the fridge before shopping, or trying to build better habits, FreddyFridge helps you make informed decisions with the food you already have.

Start with a few products, check the app regularly, and turn expiry dates into timely reminders instead of forgotten food.
